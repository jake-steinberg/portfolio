Freelance commissions

Alongside my job at the Star Tribune I take on a small number of freelance commissions each year. If you have something in mind, I’d like to hear about it.

The kind of work I take on:

  • Print reference and trail maps
  • Wall, poster and trailhead maps
  • Analytical and data-driven maps for reports and publications
  • 3D terrain and relief
  • Illustrated and hand-drawn maps

To get started, send me a note describing the place, the audience and roughly when you need it. I’ll come back with a quote, a timeline and anything I need from you. Every project starts with a short call and a written quote; rates depend on scope.
