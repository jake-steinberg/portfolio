Jake Steinberg, cartographer and data journalist: portfolio

I’m a Minneapolis-based cartographer on the graphics team at the Minnesota Star Tribune, where I collaborate with reporters, developers, and designers to advance data-driven visual storytelling. I make maps that allow people to navigate, discover, and understand the world around them. My interdisciplinary career spans news, web cartography, book publishing and trailhead map design. I’m also the author of The Twin Cities in 50 Maps and an editor for the Atlas of Design.

The Twin Cities in 50 Maps

Stories of glaciers and gangsters; epic floods and titans of industry; Native people and immigrants from every corner of the globe. The maps in this book bridge the past and present and even offer glimpses at the future. Whether you’ve lived in the Twin Cities your whole life or only just arrived, it will show you a new way to see your home.

The world in relief

Lindsey Vonn’s Mountains Star Tribune, 2026

A biography of Lindsey Vonn published in the run-up to the 2026 Winter Olympics, told by the mountains that made her. Six places on two continents (Lake Louise, Whistler and Buck Hill in North America; Åre, Cortina and Schladming in Europe), drawn as oblique terrain maps that share one lettering system: peak labels on leader lines with elevations, dashed resort boundaries, and a north arrow turned to the view.
