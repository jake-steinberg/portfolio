Howdy!

I’m a cartographer and data journalist on the graphics team at the Minnesota Star Tribune, author of The Twin Cities in 50 Maps and an editor for the Atlas of Design.

What I do

Transform data into visual stories that illuminate and inspire. My work explores hidden systems, stunning landscapes, outdoor adventures and the changing climate.

What I make

Thoughtfully designed maps and data viz for the news, the web, the bookshelf and the trailhead. I occasionally take on freelance commissions.
