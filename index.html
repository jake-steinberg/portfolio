Jake Steinberg, cartographer and data journalist: portfolio

I’m a Minneapolis-based cartographer on the graphics team at the Minnesota Star Tribune, where I collaborate with reporters, developers and designers to advance data-driven visual storytelling. I make maps that allow people to navigate, discover and understand the world around them. My interdisciplinary career spans news, web cartography, book publishing and trailhead map design. I’m also the author of The Twin Cities in 50 Maps and an editor for the Atlas of Design.

The Twin Cities in 50 Maps

Stories of glaciers and gangsters; epic floods and titans of industry; Native people and immigrants from every corner of the globe. The maps in this book bridge the past and present and even offer glimpses of the future. Whether you’ve lived in the Twin Cities your whole life or only just arrived, it will show you a new way to see your home.

The cartographer’s touch

  • Made by a cyclist for cyclists. All the info you need for a good ride. Nothing you don’t.

  • Mining landscapes rendered in beautiful shaded relief.

  • Elevation profile, so you know how far you have left—and what it will feel like.

  • High-contrast design, so the important parts hold up in bright sun.
